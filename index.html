Experience across industries

Working across different industries exposed me to a variety of data challenges, operating models, and customer needs while reinforcing common principles of product development and execution. Adapting to new domains became a core skill, and a longstanding interest in AI has evolved from theory into a practical focus as new capabilities reshape how products are built and decisions are made.

03

Where I am today

Today, I lead data platform initiatives at Zelis, focusing on the systems that move, standardize, and operationalize data across the organization. My work sits at the intersection of product, engineering, and business strategy, where reliable data infrastructure enables better decisions and faster execution. While data has become my primary area of expertise, I remain most interested in solving meaningful problems and building products that create lasting value.

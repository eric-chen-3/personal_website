About Me

I'm a product manager focused on building data-driven products and the systems that power them. My work has spanned healthcare, fintech, travel, advertising, and gaming, with a consistent focus on turning complex data challenges into scalable solutions that create value for both businesses and users.

My path to product management began at UC Berkeley, where I studied Computer Science and developed a fascination with systems thinking. Early experiences in consulting, startups, and product roles taught me how technical decisions influence business outcomes, and how the best products are built by connecting the two.

Today, I lead data platform initiatives at Zelis, where I work across product, engineering, and business teams to improve how data is ingested, standardized, and operationalized. Recently, I've become particularly interested in how AI can reshape product development, accelerate decision-making, and unlock new ways of building software.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me traveling across Asia and Europe, researching investment ideas, learning music production, or keeping up with sports. I'm especially interested in markets, technology, and the ways new ideas spread across industries and cultures.

Products I Love

These products shape how I think about data, AI, platform design, and product craft.

Data cloud

Snowflake

Snowflake has evolved from a data warehouse into a platform where data, analytics, and AI work together. Cortex extends that vision by bringing AI-powered search, analysis, and assistance directly to governed business data, showing how enterprise software can embrace AI while maintaining trust and usability.

Why I love it Having spent much of my career on data platforms, I admire products that solve complex technical problems without exposing users to the complexity underneath. Snowflake's vision of making data more accessible closely aligns with how I think great products should be built.

Fintech

Robinhood

Robinhood transformed investing by making financial markets more accessible and intuitive. As AI becomes a larger part of the experience, products like Cortex help investors process information faster and make more informed decisions.

Why I love it I spend a lot of my free time investing and following markets, so I'm naturally drawn to products that help people better understand businesses and opportunities. Robinhood continues to set a high bar for simplicity in a category where trust and clarity matter most.

Creator economy

Twitch

Twitch is fascinating because the product operates in real time. Content, chat, discovery, moderation, and monetization all work together to create a constantly evolving experience. Its recommendation systems help viewers find communities while helping creators grow their audiences.

Why I love it I've always been drawn to products with strong network effects and passionate communities. Twitch demonstrates that successful platforms are built not just around content, but around the relationships that keep users engaged.

Personalization

Spotify

Spotify is one of the best examples of personalization at scale. Features like Discover Weekly, AI DJ, and personalized recommendations make music discovery feel effortless while adapting to millions of unique listening habits.

Why I love it As someone learning music production, I spend a lot of time exploring new artists, genres, and sounds. Spotify constantly inspires me with how effectively it turns data into experiences users genuinely enjoy.
